## Editorial decisions

The brand growth workbook calls its numbers **expected results**. The website therefore explicitly labels those pages as illustrative growth plans and the figures as targets. It does not claim these are verified client achievements. No fabricated testimonials, customer logos, client counts, retention percentages or traffic metrics are published.

Where the visual mockup conflicts with the strategy workbook, the detailed workbook takes precedence. The coaching example is Bright Future Academy in Jaipur. The mockup's additional client examples are not presented as verified clients. The later user-authored upgrade brief explicitly requested business-loan assistance; that page describes preparation and guidance only, with no lender identity, approval guarantee, eligibility promise or invented terms.

The startup kit's `[Your Number]` is not published. The supplied email, hello@mybrandsbuddy.com, is used. Social handles in the launch roadmap are setup suggestions and are not linked as verified accounts.

Package fees and deliverables follow the startup kit: Starter ₹8,000/month, Growth ₹18,000/month and Scale ₹35,000/month. Advertising spend is separate, and proposals confirm taxes and scope. Speculative free-client acquisition advice is not represented as a public ongoing discount.

The three blog articles are original, practical expansions of the supplied topics and growth framework. They avoid fabricated success stories, invented publication dates and ranking guarantees.

The reference montage is not used as a flattened page or republished as client photography. The rocket and card artwork are implemented as local vector/CSS visuals.
